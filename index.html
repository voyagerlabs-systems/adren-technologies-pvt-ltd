Products

Two platforms. One foundation.

In development

Adren Travel

An AI-native booking platform for travel agents, agencies and consultant networks. Search hotels, flights, cruises and more through one conversation, under your own brand.

  • AI assistant as the front door to search, with classic forms beneath it.
  • Three experiences: sign-in, an admin console for the agency and a consultant workspace.
  • White-label by default, with automatic contrast checks on brand colors.
  • Built-in marketing tools, through ADREN or the agency's own ad account.
  • Coming next: car hire and bus booking.
Founding partner pilot

Adren Backoffice

The paperwork side of a travel wholesaler in one place: quotes, invoices, vouchers and supplier reconciliation, with multi-currency handled correctly from the first booking.

  • Quote to voucher, with deposit and final invoices.
  • Every amount tagged with its currency, and the exchange rate locked to each invoice.
  • Supplier reconciliation per booking, so the real margin is visible.
  • Your tax details (GST, VAT or other) on every invoice.
  • Multi-tenant from day one, with separate data and branding for each business.

More platforms are planned in energy, adtech, healthcare and fashion. These are directions we intend to explore, not launch dates.

Founder journey

A name that waited twenty years for a company.

The name ADREN came from a school notebook. Most of the founder's classmates had English and foreign names, while his own name was Indian, and he wanted one just as distinct. He searched online for something unique that started with the letter A, settled on ADREN, and began writing it in notebooks and drawing books everywhere.

Years later, after early ventures in fashion and food taught him how businesses really work, the idea of an AI-first technology company took shape. Today ADREN is a trademark and a company.

  • School

    The name is invented. A one-word identity, drawn over and over in notebooks.

  • 2019

    First attempt at a brand. A fashion brand begins as a college project. It does not succeed, and teaches the founder how much execution matters.

  • After college

    A food business. The founder launches Chatak Matak. It continues to run today under new ownership.

  • Nov 2025

    The idea takes shape. A plan for AI-native products across industries.

  • 2026

    ADREN Technologies Private Limited. The company is incorporated, registered with Startup India and MSME, and begins building its first products. ADREN™ is its trademark.
